fundacad.dev used to open straight into the manual. It now has a front page of its own, a [download page](../../download/) that picks the right file for your computer from the latest beta, and this news page.

The manual is still here, under **Docs** at the top of every page.

News comes as an [Atom feed](../feed.xml) too, if you would rather follow it in a reader.
