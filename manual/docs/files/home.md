# Home

Home is where FundaCAD starts. It lists the documents you worked on, starts new ones and takes you back to any version of a document.

![Home with Blank and Open at the top, three recent documents and the examples under them](../img/home.png)

## Start a document

- **Blank** starts an empty document.
- **Open…** opens a document on disk, or a STEP, STL or 3MF file.
- **An example** starts a document that already has parameters and a few steps. Save then asks where to keep it. [Examples](../start/examples.md) says what each one shows.

Before you have any documents, Home shows a short welcome and puts the examples on top, under Start here. Later they sit under your documents, under Examples.

## Open a document

Click a card to open the document. Each card shows a picture of the model from its last save, its folder and when it was last edited.

A card can carry a mark:

- **Open**: the document is open now.
- **Not found**: the file was moved or deleted. Click the card to take it off the list.
- **Could not be read**: the file is damaged or is not a document.

Right click a card for Open, Versions, Remove from list and Copy path. The pictures are kept on this computer, so a document copied from elsewhere shows its picture after you open it once.

## Search and sort

Type in Search documents, or press Ctrl+F, to find a document by name or folder, or an example by name. Esc in the search field clears it.

The list next to it sorts by Last opened, Last edited or Name.

## Folders

Add folder… puts a folder of your own under Folders, to keep the documents of one project together. Home lists the documents in it and in its subfolders, and Save offers that folder for a document you start there.

To take a folder off, right click it and choose Remove from Home. The folder stays on disk.

## Versions

The Versions button on a card lists the document's branches and saved versions.

- **Open**, next to a branch, opens the document on that branch.
- **Restore** brings a version back into the document, and Ctrl+Z undoes it.
- **Open copy** starts a new untitled document from a version, so the original file stays as it is.

## Leave Home

Press Esc or click the close button to go back to your document. File > Home and the home button in the title bar bring Home back. While Home is open, the rest of the title bar is switched off.

Turn off Show Home on startup to start straight on an empty document. After an update, Home also shows what is new in that version.

## Background

Home sits on the theme's paper. In Preferences > Appearance, Home background can be Workspace, your model dimmed behind Home, or Your own image, a PNG or JPEG you choose.
