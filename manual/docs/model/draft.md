# Draft and thread

Draft tilts a face so a wall leans back a little, and Thread adds a real helical thread to a round face.

<!-- shot: draft-thread | drafted wall beside a threaded shank -->

For Draft, click the face, drag or type an angle up to 60 degrees, and press Enter. The pull direction is Z, and X cycles through X, Y and Z.

For Thread, click the round face, then set the pitch in the P box and the length in the L box. A thread needs at least one full turn. Afterwards the thread lists Hand, Starts and Print clearance under it in the history. Edit the hole or shaft size and the thread follows it.

For a standard size, use Standard Thread from the Fasteners plugin.
