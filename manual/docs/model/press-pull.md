# Press/Pull

Press/Pull (Q) moves a face in or out, so the body grows or shrinks with it. It is the quickest way to make a wall 2 mm thicker.

![A selected top face being pulled out by Press/Pull, the value box above it](../img/presspull-face.png)


![A face being pulled out with Press/Pull](../img/presspull.gif)

Pick a flat face and drag the arrow, or type a distance, where a negative number cuts, then press Enter. Letting go of the arrow keeps the tool open with the distance in its box, so you can still change the options there before Enter, or press Esc to drop it. Ctrl+click adds faces, and T lets you click another face to stop at.

Faces that run smoothly into the moved one come along. On a round face, such as the wall of a hole, you type a radius or diameter.
