# Ready-made shapes

Primitive offers a box, cylinder, cone, sphere and torus.

![A cylinder on the origin and its Radius and Height rows in the History card](../img/primitive-cylinder.png)

Each one appears centred on the origin, and you change its sizes in the value rows of its step. Primitives suit parts made of boxes and rods, and they make good cutting tools. Put a cylinder where a hole should go, then use Subtract.
