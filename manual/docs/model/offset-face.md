# Offset face

Offset Face moves faces along their normals while the body stays closed, and the neighbouring faces stretch to follow.

<!-- shot: offset-face | block face offset outward -->

Click one or more faces, drag or type the distance, and press Enter. Letting go of the arrow keeps the tool open with the distance in its box. A single round face is resized instead, and you type a radius or diameter. It works on flat and cylindrical faces only.
