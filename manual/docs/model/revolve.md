# Revolve

Revolve spins a profile around an axis, which makes bottles, knobs and pulleys.

![A vase made by revolving a half outline](../img/revolve-profile.png)

1. Draw half the outline on one side of the axis.
2. Choose Revolve and click the profile.
3. Click an axis arrow at the origin, or a straight edge on the model, to spin around.

A datum axis selected beforehand is used at once. The revolve starts as a full turn and a new body.

## Change it later

The step's rows change it after it is made.

- Angle: how far it turns, 360 degrees by default.
- Pitch: how far one turn climbs, for a spring or a coil. Empty means a flat revolve.
- Operation: New body, Join, Cut or Intersect.
- Axis: the X, Y or Z axis of the origin.

Double-click the revolve to drag it on the model. The straight arrow sets the pitch and the curved one the angle, so ten turns is 3600 degrees.
